## The prompt

**Tell me about a time you disagreed with a teammate or manager.**

Interviewers listen for whether you can disagree productively while protecting delivery and relationships.

## Senior approach

```text
Clarify the goal
  ↓
Surface options + trade-offs
  ↓
Use data / spike / risk analysis
  ↓
Seek shared decision criteria
  ↓
Commit and own the outcome
```

Disagreement is not a personality contest. It is a decision-quality process.

## What good sounds like

- You restated the shared objective
- You acknowledged the other option’s strengths
- You proposed a cheap experiment when stuck
- You escalated with options, not complaints
- After the decision, you executed without sabotage

## Example outline

1. **Stakes:** migration timeline vs reliability risk  
2. **Disagreement:** ship big-bang vs strangler migration  
3. **Action:** wrote a one-pager with failure modes and cost; ran a 3-day spike  
4. **Result:** team chose incremental cutover; incident rate stayed flat; date slipped one week with stakeholder buy-in  

## Influence tools seniors use

| Tool | When |
|---|---|
| Written RFC | Ambiguous multi-team decisions |
| Spike | Opinions without evidence |
| Risk register | Executives need downside clarity |
| Customer impact framing | Priority fights |

## Mistakes to avoid

- Winning the argument and losing the team
- Silent disagreement then “I told you so”
- Escalating as a first move
- Refusing to commit after dissent
