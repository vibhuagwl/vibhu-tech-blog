## What “leadership” means in engineering interviews

Leadership is not a title. It is the ability to create clarity, raise the quality bar, grow others, and align technical work to business outcomes.

## Mentorship story checklist

- Specific engineer and starting gap
- Coaching mechanism (pairing, review standards, goals)
- Observable growth
- What you changed in your own style

Avoid generic “I mentored juniors.” Name the system you built around mentoring.

## Stakeholder communication

Translate:

| Audience | They need |
|---|---|
| Engineers | Design constraints and interfaces |
| PM | Scope, risk, date confidence |
| Exec | Impact, options, recommendation |

A strong story shows you changed the message without changing the truth.

## Leadership under ambiguity

```text
Unknown requirements
  ↓
Frame options
  ↓
Time-box discovery
  ↓
Make a reversible decision
  ↓
Re-evaluate with new data
```

## Example prompt answers to prepare

1. Time you raised the engineering bar
2. Time you coached someone through a hard growth area
3. Time you said no to a feature with a better path
4. Time you aligned two orgs on a shared platform bet

## Closing signal

End with business outcome and people outcome. Senior leadership moves both systems and humans forward.
