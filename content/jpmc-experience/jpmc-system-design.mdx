### Example skeleton — Kafka replay platform
Requirements: controlled reprocess, authz, audit, rate limit, dry-run, reports.  
Components: selector API, validator, publisher, audit store, metrics.  
Risks: duplicates, ordering, overload.  
Tradeoff: replay to original topic vs dedicated replay topic.
