## Pyramid (spoken)

Unit for rules/validators · Integration for DB/Kafka adapters · Contract for APIs · Selective E2E for critical journeys · Failure tests for retries/DLQ

## What to mock vs real
Mock unstable external tax/GraphQL in CI with WireMock. Prefer real embedded/local Kafka or testcontainers when failure semantics matter (`[NEEDS MY REAL PROJECT DETAIL]` what you used).

## Must-test Kafka behaviors
Duplicate delivery · retry then DLQ · ordering within partition · consumer restart · poison message isolation

## DB rollback
Assert transactional boundaries: failed processing does not leave partial business state without compensation.
