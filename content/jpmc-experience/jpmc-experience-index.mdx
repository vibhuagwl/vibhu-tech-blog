## How to use this hub

Answer interview questions with **ownership language**:

> What problem were we solving, why this design, what I built, what could fail, how we made it production-ready.

Do **not** invent volumes, latency numbers, team size, or tools you did not use. Fill placeholders marked `[NEEDS MY REAL PROJECT DETAIL]` before interviews.
