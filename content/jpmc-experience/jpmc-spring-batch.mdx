## Model

Job → Step → ItemReader → ItemProcessor → ItemWriter · chunk commit interval · JobRepository

## Tradeoff
100k records with chunk 10 vs 10k: smaller chunks = more commits/overhead; larger = longer transactions / bigger rollback units.

## Failure
Restart from checkpoint; skip policy for bad records with audit; never silently drop financial rows without policy.
