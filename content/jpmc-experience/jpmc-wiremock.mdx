## Why WireMock
External tax/partner APIs are not reliable CI dependencies. We stub success and failure shapes.

## Scenarios to demo in interview
Success · timeout · 500 · 400 · invalid body · slow response · duplicate callbacks

Prove: retry only on retryable; permanent errors go to fail/DLQ path; timeouts don’t hang threads unboundedly.
