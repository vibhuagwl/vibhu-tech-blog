## Why it mattered

Swagger/OpenAPI made the **contract** explicit: DTOs, required fields, error shapes, auth — so consumers and testers weren’t reverse-engineering controllers.

## What good looks like
- Request/response models (not raw domain entities)
- Documented 4xx/5xx
- Auth scheme documented
- Versioning strategy (`[NEEDS MY REAL PROJECT DETAIL]`)

## Interview angle
> We didn’t expose internal entities. DTOs protected the domain model and let us evolve persistence without breaking clients.
