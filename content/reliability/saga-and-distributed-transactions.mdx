## Story

Order service succeeds, payment succeeds, inventory fails. Welcome to distributed reality.

## Prefer local ACID first

If one database can own the invariant, keep the transaction local. Distributed transactions are operational gravity.

## 2PC

All-or-nothing across resources. Blocking, coupling, painful under partitions. Rarely the interview “happy default.”

## Saga

Sequence of local transactions with **compensations** on failure.

```text
CreateOrder → Pay → ReserveInventory → Ship
Fail at Ship → cancel shipment intent / release inventory / refund
```

### Orchestration

A coordinator drives steps.

### Choreography

Events trigger the next step (emergent, harder to see globally).

## Payment sketch

Use outbox for step events; idempotent handlers; compensation that is itself idempotent; never “exactly once” hand-waving.

## Remember This

1. Local transactions beat distributed fantasy.  
2. Saga = progress + compensation.  
3. Compensation must be safe to retry.  
4. Outbox prevents dual-write loss.  
5. Say RPO-like expectations for each step’s visibility.
