## The reasoning pattern they want

**Duplicate payment example (memorize the chain):**

```text
Kafka retry
  → duplicate delivery (at-least-once)
  → idempotency key on payment
  → DB unique constraint
  → transactional processing (DB)
  → outbox / publish after commit
  → offset commit after side effects (or transactional consume pattern)
  → recovery / DLQ for poison
```

Kafka **exactly-once** ≠ your **payment system** is exactly-once across Kafka + DB + PSP.
