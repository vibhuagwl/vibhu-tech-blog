## Category

Consumer

## Purpose

Limits how many records a single `poll()` may return.

## Why it matters

Each poll’s records must be processable before `max.poll.interval.ms`.

```text
poll batch too large + slow processing
        ↓
miss poll interval
        ↓
rebalance / lag amplification
```

## When to increase

- Processing is fast and cheap
- You want fewer poll cycles and higher throughput
- Downstream can absorb bursts

## When to decrease

- Per-record work is heavy (HTTP, DB, locks)
- Rebalances during lag spikes
- You need tighter control of processing bursts

## Trade-off

```text
Higher max.poll.records → potential throughput ↑, rebalance risk ↑ if slow
Lower max.poll.records  → safer poll loop, more overhead
```

## Related settings

- `max.poll.interval.ms`
- `session.timeout.ms` / heartbeats
- commit strategy (`enable.auto.commit` vs explicit)

## Real-world story

See [Consumer lag incident](/kafka-interview/kafka-experience-consumer-lag) — reducing poll size was part of stabilizing a slow downstream bank API path.

## Interview tip

“`max.poll.records` is not ‘speed.’ It is the size of the work unit you must finish between polls.”
