## Kafka — 1 Minute Revision

**What is Kafka?**  
→ Distributed event streaming platform.

**Why partitions?**  
→ Parallelism and scalability.

**Why consumer groups?**  
→ Parallel consumption with shared progress per group.

**Why replication?**  
→ Fault tolerance.

**Why ISR?**  
→ Track replicas that are sufficiently caught up for durability/failover.

**What causes consumer lag?**  
→ Processing slower than the incoming rate (or rebalance / poison loops).

**First things to check?**  
→ Lag → processing time → partitions → consumers → downstream dependencies.
