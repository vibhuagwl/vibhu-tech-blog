## What this section is for

**Learn** how Kafka works before you optimize, configure, or defend it in an interview.

Do not start with a 200-page mega article.

Use this map. Open one focused topic at a time.

## Learning path

```text
Why Kafka exists
      ↓
Architecture (broker → topic → partition → replica)
      ↓
Producer path
      ↓
Consumer groups & offsets
      ↓
Replication / ISR / controller
      ↓
Delivery semantics
      ↓
Internals that matter in production
```

## Concept checklist

Work through these concepts in order. Prefer short, story-driven pages over giant dumps.

1. What problem Kafka solves
2. Topics and partitions
3. Brokers and replicas
4. Leaders / followers / ISR
5. Producers and partitioning
6. Consumers and consumer groups
7. Offsets and commit strategies
8. Replication and durability
9. Controller / KRaft
10. Retention and log segments
11. Delivery semantics (at-most / at-least / exactly-once)
12. Rebalancing and assignment
13. Dead letter / poison messages
14. Transactions (when needed)
