## 20-second interview version

1. Find active queries / blockers  
2. Deadlocks & long transactions  
3. `EXPLAIN ANALYZE` + missing indexes  
4. Pool exhaustion (Hikari)  
5. Batch big writes · prefer keyset pagination  
6. Fix → deploy → watch p95/p99, locks, lag  

## Remember

Identify the bottleneck first. A fast plan beats a brave rewrite.
