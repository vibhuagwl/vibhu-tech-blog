## 20-second interview version

- Browser → gateway: normal HTTPS is usually enough  
- Payment ↔ bank / money services: **mTLS** (both sides prove identity with certificates)  
- Keystore = who I am · Truststore = who I trust  
- Automate renewals (cert-manager / Vault / ACM) or certs expire and outages follow  

## Remember

mTLS is for **critical trust**, not every chatty internal call.
