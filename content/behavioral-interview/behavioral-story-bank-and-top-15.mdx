## Do not memorize 260 answers

At this level, interviewers reuse the same signals. Build a **story bank**, then map any question to a story + angle.

```text
                    YOUR STORY BANK
                          │
       ┌──────────────────┼──────────────────┐
       ↓                  ↓                  ↓
  Transformation     Cost Saving       Production Crisis
       │                  │                  │
       ↓                  ↓                  ↓
 Monolith → MS       $1M+ Saving       P1 Incident
       │                  │                  │
       └──────────────────┼──────────────────┘
                          ↓
                 Leadership Stories
                          │
       ┌──────────────────┼──────────────────┐
       ↓                  ↓                  ↓
   Conflict           Mentoring          Innovation
       │                  │                  │
       ↓                  ↓                  ↓
 Architecture        Team Growth       Automation/Tooling
       │                  │                  │
       └──────────────────┼──────────────────┘
                          ↓
                 Business Impact
```

## Recommended story cards (Vibhu-aligned)

| Story ID | Headline | Best for | Result to cite |
|---|---|---|---|
| T1 | Monolith → microservices + Kafka | Transformation, architecture, influence | Faster releases, ownership, 99.9% paths |
| C1 | Splunk → custom analytics | Cost, strategy, status quo | **$1M+/yr** savings |
| P1 | API/query/cache redesign | Performance, measurement | **−60%** latency, **+70%** throughput |
| R1 | Kafka DLQ Replay Tool | Ownership, incidents, invent & simplify | Recovery **−50%** |
| O1 | Cross-team contracts before build | Failure, stakeholder alignment | Fewer integration surprises |
| M1 | Mentoring → design ownership | Mentoring, succession | Engineers present/defend designs |
| D1 | Disagree with timeline / architecture | Conflict, exec communication | Phased delivery / hybrid design |
| A1 | AI-assisted engineering (Copilot/Cursor) with guardrails | Innovation, AI, modernity | Faster delivery without money-path AI guesswork |
| S1 | Security/compliance (OAuth2, PCI-conscious paths) | Security & risk | Compliance without blocking delivery |
| L1 | Leading 4 engineers + architecture forums | Team building, standards | Consistent delivery quality |

For each card, write a **90-second** and **4-minute** version. Always end with learning + lasting control.

## Mapping any question in 10 seconds

1. Name the **signal** (ownership, conflict, cost…).
2. Pick the **story card**.
3. Emphasize the angle they asked (people vs tech vs business).
4. Close with metric + prevention/learning.
