## 1. Tell me about yourself

**Answer**

I am a technology leader with extensive experience building and modernizing large-scale financial and distributed systems.

Over my career, I have worked across Java, Spring Boot, microservices, Kafka, cloud platforms, distributed systems, payments, and high-volume transaction processing.

At the senior leadership level, my focus has moved beyond writing code to solving complex business problems through technology. I have led modernization initiatives, monolith-to-microservices migrations, observability transformations, performance improvements, security initiatives, and cost-optimization programs.

One example was leading a migration from Splunk to a custom analytics platform. The objective was not simply to replace a technology but to preserve critical observability capabilities while significantly reducing infrastructure costs. The initiative resulted in more than **$1 million in annual infrastructure savings**.

I would describe my leadership style as hands-on when necessary, but primarily focused on architecture, technical direction, mentoring, stakeholder alignment, and delivering measurable business outcomes.

**Delivery tip:** 90 seconds. One flagship metric. End on how you lead now — not a chronological resume dump.

## 2. Tell me about a time you demonstrated leadership

**Situation:** A large financial system’s legacy monolith created deployment dependencies and slowed delivery across teams.

**Task:** Own a migration strategy toward independently deployable microservices — without a reckless rewrite.

**Action:**
- Identified business capabilities that could be separated incrementally
- Introduced service boundaries, Kafka-based communication, APIs, observability, security, and independent pipelines
- Spent significant time aligning multiple teams — the hardest part was organizational, not technical

**Result:** More scalable architecture, faster deployment cycles, clearer team ownership, reduced coupling.

**Close:** Leadership is not making the architecture decision alone. It is creating alignment so multiple teams can execute it.

**Signals:** Ownership, Think Big, Influence without authority, Deliver Results.
