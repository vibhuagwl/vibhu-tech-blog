## How to answer this competency

Show you **absorb ambiguity**, create clarity, and leave durable ownership — not heroic firefighting alone.

## Flagship STAR — Largest initiative / personally driven (Q1, Q15)

**S:** Legacy financial platform slowed delivery and scaled poorly.  
**T:** Drive incremental modernization to independently deployable services.  
**A:** Capability map → strangler slices → Kafka boundaries → pipelines/obs/security → multi-team alignment.  
**R:** Faster cycles, clearer ownership, reduced coupling.  
**Close:** Largest initiatives succeed when org alignment matches architecture.

## Q2 — Problem nobody wanted

Use **R1 (DLQ Replay)**: gap between platform/payments/ops; no clear owner; you made the business case and shipped validated replay → recovery −50%.

## Q3 — Inherited failing project

Stabilize customer impact → reset scope/risk visibly → rebuild contracts and checkpoints → deliver phased value → install prevention (reviews, SLIs).

## Q4 — Turn around struggling team

Diagnose (skills vs clarity vs overload) → standards + ownership slices → design forums → coaching loops → measure delivery consistency.

## Q5–Q6 — Lead / influence without authority

Document problem, options, migration, per-team impact; invite challenge; adopt via credibility (story **T1/O1**).

## Q7–Q9 — Challenge leadership / unpopular / multi-org decisions

Present options with risk/cost/customer impact (not “can’t”). Example: timeline pushback → phased delivery (**D1**). Cost status-quo challenge → analytics migration (**C1**).

## Q10 — Accountability for someone else’s mistake

Own the system outcome in the incident/bridge; protect the person; fix process (contracts, tests, ownership matrix). Blame ≠ leadership.

## Q11–Q12 — Beyond role / decision nobody wanted

Ship tooling/runbooks that sit between teams; make the call on durability vs availability with explicit trade-offs.

## Q13–Q14 — Cross-boundary ownership gaps

RACI for interfaces, event contracts, on-call ownership, architecture forums; prevent “thrown over the wall” with shared SLIs and DLQ ownership.
