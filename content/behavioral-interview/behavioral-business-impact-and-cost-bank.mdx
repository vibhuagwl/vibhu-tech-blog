## Flagship — Measurable value / largest cost save (Q49, Q52, Q56, Q63–Q64, Q67)

**C1 Splunk → custom analytics:** **$1M+/yr**. Phased migration; preserve security/observability; parallel run; cut unused ingest.

## Q50–Q55 — Revenue, CX, efficiency, automation

Frame platform availability/throughput as enabling higher transaction volume (**P1**, 99.9%). Onboarding APIs +35% faster as CX/ops efficiency. Playwright automation as quality+speed. Kafka reporting −90% time as customer/merchant CX.

## Q57–Q62 — ROI language

ROI = (savings or revenue protection + risk avoided − build/run cost) / investment, with confidence ranges. Exec pitch: cost, risk, timeline, customer impact — not codec lists. Defend investment in cuts by tying to regulatory/availability risk.

## Q65–Q73 — Cloud waste, vendors, productivity, risk

Find waste via unused retention, over-provision, duplicate tooling. Challenge expensive vendor with capability matrix + migration risk. Cost vs quality: never strip audit trails for money. Efficiency metrics: $/txn, lag age, deploy frequency, escaped defects, cache hit ratio, idle capacity.
