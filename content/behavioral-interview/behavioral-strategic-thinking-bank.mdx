## Signal

You connect **tech bets → business outcomes → risk → time horizon**.

## Flagship — Strategy that changed direction (Q16–Q18, Q26)

**C1 / T1:** Observability cost trajectory and monolith coupling were becoming business constraints. Strategy: phased platform modernization + cost architecture, not fashion rewrites. Multi-year impact: lower run-rate, faster change.

## Q19–Q20 — Investment recommended vs rejected

Recommend when ROI + risk clear (analytics migration). Reject shiny platform that duplicates Kafka bus or adds ops without customer value.

## Q21 — Build / buy / reuse

Criteria: differentiation, compliance, TCO, talent, lock-in, time-to-value. Buy commodity; build where it creates edge or cost leverage (custom analytics).

## Q22–Q23 — Simplify / prevent over-engineering

Prefer SQS+replay for dominant tax errors over heavy workflow everywhere; templates over gold-plated frameworks.

## Q24 — Short-term vs long-term

Two-way door now (guarded manual replay) + one-way durable tool in same milestone (**R1**).

## Q25, Q28–Q29 — Risks & trends

Identify risks via lag/ISR/cost curves/SLO debt. Reject trends lacking problem fit (e.g., replacing Kafka with Redis Streams for payment backbone).

## Q27, Q30 — Adoption & modernization success

Adoption via RFCs + pilots. Success ≠ “migrated”: measure cost, latency, deploy frequency, escaped defects, ownership clarity, customer outcomes.
