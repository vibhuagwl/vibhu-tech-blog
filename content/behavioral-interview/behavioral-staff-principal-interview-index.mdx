## Who this is for

At **25+ YOE**, interviewers assess whether you operate as a **Principal / Distinguished Engineer / Architect / Engineering Leader** — not whether you can recite HR scripts.

They listen for strategic thinking, ownership, executive communication, conflict resolution, mentoring/succession, architecture judgment, business impact, ambiguity, failure, cost optimization, influence without authority, large transformations, and customer/business focus.

## Start here

1. Build your **[Story Bank & Top 15](/behavioral-interview/behavioral-story-bank-and-top-15)** (10–12 cards, not 260 memorized answers).
2. Drill the classic **[30 STAR set](/behavioral-interview/behavioral-intro-and-leadership)** for fluency.
3. Expand by competency using the banks below when a prompt is unfamiliar.

## How to deliver STAR at this level

```text
Situation  → business stakes (customers, money, risk)
Task       → YOUR ownership
Action     → decisions, options rejected, alignment
Result     → metrics + lasting org/system change + learning
```
