## Who this is for

At **25+ YOE / Staff+ / Principal / Architect**, behavioral rounds score **engineering leadership**, not task narration.

Interviewers listen for:

| Signal | What it sounds like |
|---|---|
| Strategic thinking | Business + architecture, not only code |
| Ownership | You stayed until outcomes and prevention |
| Executive communication | Impact, risk, cost, timeline |
| Conflict & influence | Evidence over ego; influence without authority |
| Mentoring / succession | Org stronger without you as bottleneck |
| Ambiguity & failure | Reversible vs irreversible; accountability |
| Measurable impact | Latency, cost ($1M+), delivery risk |

## How to deliver STAR at this level

```text
Situation  → business stakes (customers, money, risk)
Task       → YOUR ownership (not “the team vaguely”)
Action     → decisions, options rejected, alignment
Result     → metrics + lasting org/system change + learning
```

Spend most time on **Action + Result**. Close by naming the leadership principle or signal you demonstrated.
