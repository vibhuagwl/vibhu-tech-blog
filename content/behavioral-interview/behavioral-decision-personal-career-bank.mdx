## Decision making (Q216–Q225)

Hardest decisions combine irreversible + incomplete data + money/risk. Use reversible/irreversible split; time-box analysis; write the decision; assign execution owner; revisit with metrics. Financial consequence example: **C1**. Affecting many engineers: platform standards via forums. No obvious right answer: experiment criteria. Avoid paralysis: default to reversible action. Ensure execution: owners, dates, review. Do differently: earlier stakeholder contracts (**O1**).

## Personal leadership (Q226–Q240)

**Style:** clarity, ownership, trust; hands-on when risk demands.  
**Strengths:** systems judgment, stakeholder translation, ownership.  
**Weakness:** historically solving too much yourself — fixed via mentoring/delegation.  
**Best environment:** high standards + psychological safety.  
**Best manager:** clear priorities, debate welcome.  
**Criticism/rejection:** extract signal; don’t defend ego.  
**Stress:** stabilize → prioritize → communicate.  
**Motivate:** impact at tech×business intersection.  
**Frustrates:** hidden risk and fake certainty.  
**Proud:** org capability after you leave the room.  
**Leadership/integrity/values:** truth over comfort; no silent money risk.  
**Lesson:** enable others; evidence over hierarchy.
