## AI & modern tech (Q166–Q177)

**How AI changes engineering:** accelerates boilerplate and exploration; raises bar on review, architecture, threat modeling, and verification.

**Your use:** Copilot/Cursor/Claude for coding assistance; never unverified AI into payment ledgers.

**Enterprise AI safely:** private models/endpoints where needed; DLP; allowlists; human review for prod; audit prompts/outputs on sensitive systems.

**Risks:** secret leakage, hallucinated security, license issues, over-trust.

**Public tools:** policy by data classification — forbid customer/PCI in public LLMs.

**Productivity:** cycle time, PR size/quality, escaped defects — not LOC.

**Adopt AI for business:** RAG/assistant for ops runbooks with citations; measure deflection + accuracy.

**Build vs buy AI:** differentiate vs commodity; data gravity; compliance.

**Skills that matter more:** problem framing, verification, systems design, communication.

**Stay relevant:** use AI as leverage; deepen architecture/judgment.

**Org redesign:** AI-assisted SDLC with stronger review gates and eval harnesses.

## Security & risk (Q178–Q187)

Identify risks early (OAuth2/PCI paths). Architecture decisions: least privilege, encryption, audit. Speed vs security: don’t ship money paths without controls — phase features. Sensitive data: classification + retention. Vulnerabilities: patch SLAs, blast radius. Productivity: secure defaults/templates. Third parties: vendor review. Compliance: make requirements concrete in CI. Reject designs that dual-write secrets or skip validation on replay.
