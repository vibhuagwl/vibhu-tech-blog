## 6. Tell me about a failure

**Situation:** Technically strong initiative under-involved downstream teams early.

**What went wrong:** Integration revealed mismatched expectations on API behavior and operational responsibilities.

**Action:** Took ownership (no blame). Brought stakeholders together; documented contracts and responsibilities; established architecture checkpoints for future work.

**Result:** Project succeeded; process permanently changed.

**Close:** For cross-team work, make stakeholder alignment, API contracts, operational ownership, and failure scenarios explicit **before** implementation.

---

## 7. Biggest professional mistake

**Mistake:** As scope grew, solving every hard problem personally — creating a bottleneck and dependency.

**Change:** Delegate ownership, mentor, design reviews, engineers present/defend their own solutions.

**Result:** Stronger engineers, better knowledge distribution, team independence.

**Close:** Leadership is measured by what the organization can deliver **without you**.

## 14. Production incident

Three tracks in parallel:

| Track | Focus |
|---|---|
| **Stabilize** | Reduce customer impact; rollback / flag / fallback |
| **Diagnose** | Logs, metrics, traces, recent deploys |
| **Communicate** | Impact, mitigation, ETA for stakeholders |

After recovery: root cause + prevention (monitoring, automated recovery, deploy practices, tests, remove SPOFs).

**Close:** An incident is resolved only when immediate impact **and** systemic cause are addressed.
