## Delivery / Agile execution (Q188–Q198)

Behind schedule: re-baseline scope; critical path; daily risks; cut non-essentials. Requirements change: decision log + thin vertical slices. Scope creep: capacity-visible backlog. Unrealistic estimates: range estimates + risks. Large architecture estimates: milestones by capability, not Gantt fiction. Transformations: strangler increments (**T1**). Dependencies: contract tests + explicit owners. Quality vs speed: quality bars on money paths. Limited resources: highest customer-risk first. Production ready: SLOs, runbooks, rollback, alerts, load proof — not “tests green only.”

## Global / cross-cultural (Q199–Q207)

Multi-country: written RFCs beat hallway decisions. Distributed teams: overlap hours for decisions; async for execution. Offshore communication: over-communicate context and examples. Equal visibility: shared dashboards, rotating meeting times. Timezones: follow-the-sun on-call with crisp handoffs. Cultural misunderstanding: assume positive intent; clarify norms. Trust: deliver small commitments reliably. Prevent HQ bottleneck: local ownership + platform self-service. Multi-site architecture: documented ADRs, not verbal.

## Customer focus (Q208–Q215)

Solve customer problems via onboarding/tax visibility outcomes. Escalations: stabilize, communicate, fix, prevent. Tech→CX: reporting −90%, onboarding +35%. Prioritize by severity × reach × revenue risk. Conflict with constraints: offer safer alternative. Bad customer-requested design: educate with failure scenarios; propose compliant path. Measure: NPS/tickets/latency/error budgets. Prevent impact: canaries, validation gates, lag SLOs.
