## Per dependency

```text
Timeout → Retry+jitter (if idempotent) → Circuit → Fallback → DLQ/alert
```

## Must-say words

Idempotency · backoff · jitter · budget · bulkhead · degrade · poison message

## Never

Infinite retries · retry non-idempotent payments · ignore lag · no timeouts
